Unit 2 · Our Solar System as a System · Do this on your own.

Asteroids, comets, and icy worlds are leftover building blocks from 4.6 billion years ago. They tell us how our Solar System formed.

How this works

  1. Type your name below.
  2. Click a glowing area in the moving sky. Or use the buttons under it.
  3. Each stop has 4 steps: Learn, Explore, Check, Notes.
  4. Answer the questions. Try again if you miss one.
  5. Visit all 5 stops.
  6. Pick an option. Write your answers in the boxes.
  7. Get your Note Guide (you keep it).
  8. Make your Report (you turn it in on Canvas).

Your work saves on this computer. If you close the page, open it again to keep going.
